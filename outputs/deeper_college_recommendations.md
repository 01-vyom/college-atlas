## Schools To Treat Carefully

- UCSB: elite materials research, but undergraduate path is not a simple
  standalone materials major.
- Colorado School of Mines: great materials/engineering, weak biology/clinical
  fit for a medicine-centered list.
- Rice, Northeastern, Princeton: strong research/pre-med environments, but not
  materials-anchor picks from this dataset.
- Baylor, Loyola Chicago, Tulane, Miami, Boston College: consider mainly if aid
  is strong and medicine/pre-med outweighs materials.

## Final Shortlist Shape

A balanced application list should include:

- 3-5 far reaches that match the story: Stanford, Northwestern, Hopkins, UCLA,
  MIT, Duke, Penn, Harvard, Caltech.
- 4-6 reach/competitive schools with real fit: Michigan, UCSD, Berkeley,
  Georgia Tech, Case Western, UCI, UC Davis, Maryland, Texas Austin, Rochester.
- 5-8 more accessible research/value schools: Wisconsin, Ohio State, Minnesota,
  Purdue, Pitt, UC Riverside, ASU, Arizona, Utah, UConn, UC Merced, SJSU, UNR.

Before finalizing any school, verify department pages, major-level selectivity,
aid/net price, pre-health advising, hospital/clinical access, and whether
undergraduates can join relevant labs early.
